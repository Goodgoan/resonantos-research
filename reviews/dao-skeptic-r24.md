## Summary: objections for the lead to act on first

1. **D-realms-spl-governance-8 (major).** The proposed upgrade and TUF-root thresholds (3-of-5 with "at least 2 outside") let the three custodians act alone. The Squads cancel offered as the appeal route needs the same 3 votes that approved. Require insider keys ≤ k-1, or remove Vote from custodian keys, and add an insider-quorum fault test.
2. **D-realms-spl-governance-5, -6, -7 and TM-R24-1 (major).** The realm authority can swap weight plugins and quorum denominators at any time, even when it is a single key. The voting-mint authority can mint voters or revoke members mid-vote, with no DR-06 record. "One-way council removal" therefore does not end custodian control. Add these key classes to the checklist, the verifier and T12, and define transition completion as a verified state.
3. **D-realms-spl-governance-9 (major).** "Realms governs the Squads vault's membership" contradicts "autonomous, config_authority null". As written, either the community has no control over the key set or the time lock is bypassable. Pick one design and test it.

The live Realms authority has no veto at all (community veto `Disabled`, supply 0), so the expected verifier verdict names the wrong clause.
